## Executive Summary

This report analyzes water consumption, treatment efficiency, energy usage, and operational costs across **8 industrial plants** in Maharashtra over a 12-month period (2026). The analysis identifies key performance gaps, sustainability opportunities, and actionable recommendations to reduce costs and improve operational efficiency.

**Fleet-level highlights:**
- Total water consumed: ~160 million liters across all plants
- Average fleet recycling rate: **~32%** (industry target: 40%)
- Top efficiency opportunity: **Plant P004 (Aurangabad East)** — 28% above average consumption per production unit
- Quality violations concentrated in **monsoon months (July–September)**
- Estimated annual savings potential: **₹12–18 lakhs** through targeted interventions

## Finding 1: Plant P004 Is the Highest Water Consumer

> **Impact**: HIGH | **Category**: Water Efficiency

**Aurangabad East (P004)** consumed approximately **23% more water per production unit** compared to the 8-plant fleet average, despite producing an average volume of output.

| Metric | P004 | Fleet Average |
|--------|------|---------------|
| Avg Daily Consumption | ~91,000 L | ~73,000 L |
| Water per Unit Produced | ~118 L/unit | ~95 L/unit |
| Annual Excess vs Average | ~6.5 million L | — |

**Root Cause Hypothesis**: Aging treatment infrastructure or process inefficiency in the treatment line at P004.

**Recommendation**: Conduct an operational audit at P004. A 15% efficiency improvement would save approximately **₹7–9 lakh/year** in water and treatment costs.

> **Impact**: MEDIUM | **Category**: Sustainability

The fleet-wide recycling percentage improved from **~29% in January to ~38% in December**, indicating positive process improvements. However, the annual average of **~32%** remains below the 40% industry best-practice target.

**Best-performing plant**: Plant P007 (Kolhapur West) — **avg 41.2% recycling**

## Finding 3: Water Quality Violations Peak During Monsoon

> **Impact**: HIGH | **Category**: Water Quality & Compliance

Quality violations (Warning + Critical readings) increased by **~67%** during July–September compared to the annual average. Critical readings (pH < 5.5 or TDS > 1000 ppm) were concentrated in this period.

| Month | Total Readings | Critical | Warning | Violation % |
|-------|---------------|----------|---------|------------|
| Jan | 248 | 18 | 32 | 20.2% |
| Jul | 248 | 51 | 48 | 39.9% |
| Aug | 248 | 48 | 45 | 37.6% |
| Sep | 248 | 42 | 41 | 33.5% |
| Dec | 248 | 16 | 28 | 17.7% |

**Plant P004 generates 3× more quality violations than the fleet average.**

**Recommendation**:
1. Increase monitoring frequency at P004 from daily to twice-daily during monsoon season
2. Install pre-treatment filtration for runoff contamination at P003 and P004 (highest turbidity)
3. Set automated alerts when turbidity > 5 NTU or pH outside [6.5, 8.5]

## Finding 4: Energy Consumption Outpaced Production Growth in Q3

> **Impact**: MEDIUM | **Category**: Energy Efficiency

Energy consumption per production unit increased by **~8% in Q3** (July–September) despite production volume remaining stable. This suggests declining operational efficiency during monsoon months, possibly linked to additional pumping or treatment loads due to water quality degradation.

| Quarter | kWh per Unit | Change |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Q1 | 1.87 | Baseline |
| Q2 | 1.89 | +1.1% |
| Q3 | 2.02 | +8.0% |
| Q4 | 1.91 | +2.1% |

**Plant P006 (Nagpur Industrial)** is the highest absolute energy consumer but has moderate kWh/unit due to high production volume.

**Recommendation**: Investigate Q3 energy spike — likely caused by increased pumping for contaminated water re-processing. Addressing quality (Finding 3) should reduce energy costs simultaneously.

## Finding 6: Downtime Causes Significant Resource Waste

> **Impact**: MEDIUM | **Category**: Operational Continuity

Approximately **2% of operating days** across all plants experienced zero production (downtime), during which water and energy consumption continued at 60–70% of normal levels — representing pure waste.

**Estimated annual waste during downtime:**
- Water: ~450,000 liters across fleet
- Energy: ~3,200 kWh across fleet
- Cost: ~₹85,000 in direct resource costs

**Recommendation**: Implement a "standby mode" protocol for non-production days to reduce idle consumption by 40–50%.
